Magazine: August release — Issue 002, expanded Primer, and the monthly pipeline

Issue 002 "The Reckoning" (80pp): July with hindsight across four acts — the
agent-security cluster, the memory squeeze, the labour data, and the August
look-ahead. Issue 001's open predictions graded in public; two were wrong.

The Primer absorbs "How The Models Are Made" as Acts IV and V (63pp) and is the
single free flagship again. models-issue.js is retired.

SECURITY: web/data/issue-001.js still held all 59 spreads of the paid issue, and
web/ is served as static assets, so it was fetchable directly by anyone. Deleted.

Renderer fix: featureText()'s close() appended the sources footer as the last
child of the page element, so on splitLeft/splitRight — a flex row — it became a
third column and crushed the text column from 333px to 67px. Any split page
carrying sources overflowed regardless of copy length. Issue 001 p25 shipped with
this defect; the styles.css V-FIX repairs both issues.

Standard: page range corrected to 40-80 to match the enforced gate, copy budgets
replaced with values measured off Issue 001, section 14 renderer traps added, and
Law 11 (no source lines on magazine pages) added and enforced.

qa_scan.py now scans functions/api/issue/_data/*.json, which nothing had scanned
since paid issues moved there, and no longer fires on metadata stubs or on
article prose about other people's agents.

New tooling: fold_chop.py (gatefold halves), gen_issue_art.py (batch art),
devserver.py (the dev server the QA gate referenced but that never existed),
audit_reader.py (the real-reader audit), and a four-phase monthly pipeline that
begins gathering on the 25th and stops for review before anything publishes.

Art: 67 images generated through the house Nano Banana pipeline, $2.28 metered.
