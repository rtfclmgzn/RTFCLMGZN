# Acceptance Hotfix 0.3.1

This hotfix was applied after the first real draft cycle safely stopped at the per-cycle web-search ceiling.

Changes:

- Discovery and assignment use the cost-sensitive Luna profile.
- Evidence collection, verification, drafting, and editorial review retain Terra-class reasoning.
- Web search runs during discovery and evidence collection, not redundantly during signal intake and final verification.
- Discovery candidates are reduced from 12 to 6.
- The per-cycle search ceiling is 24.
- Owner daily and monthly budget limits are unchanged.
- Scheduling, automatic publication, and social posting remain disabled.

The failed acceptance record remains in the audit database. No article is published by this hotfix.
